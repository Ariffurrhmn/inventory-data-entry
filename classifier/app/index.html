How the decision is made
  1. Read the page. Local illumination correction and multiple OCR views recover printed text.
  2. Inspect explicit evidence. A unique printed heading or a conservative supply-request rule may identify the type.
  3. Use the trained fallback. Word/bigram TF-IDF and Complement Naive Bayes classify remaining cases.
  4. Expose the reason. Show matched text, or exact supporting and opposing n-gram contributions.
Read the numbers honestly
These are adaptive development results on a small dataset. The rules were refined during development. Neither figure establishes independent real-world accuracy.
Training: 150 documents. Validation: 25. The 27 historical test records were excluded from these experiments.

Scope & privacy
Four-way classification only. No table extraction, numeric extraction or inventory updates.
Handwriting is outside the model’s scope. Signatures are not transcription targets.
