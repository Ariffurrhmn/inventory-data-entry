## Lessons from rounds 1–17 (keep telling ChatGPT)

- Numbers have been 100% correct so far. Keep checking anyway.
- Vary the **handwriting** and **signer name** every time. It keeps sliding back to one neat blue hand.
- Keep text normal-size and the layout dense, not big designer headings.
- Challans: never any prices, and never "Copy", "Duplicate" or "Original".
- Customer copies: the copy marking must be clearly visible. That's the whole point of the type.
